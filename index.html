Frequently Asked Questions

Questions & Answers

  • 01. What types of transportation services do you offer?

    Answer:

    We provide a range of logistics solutions, including road freight, air freight, ocean shipping, cargo handling, warehousing, and distribution services to support different transportation requirements.

  • 02. How can I track my shipment?

    Answer:

    You can track your shipment using the tracking number provided when your order is confirmed. Our tracking support helps you follow shipment progress and stay informed about delivery updates.

  • 03. How long does international shipping take?

    Answer:

    Delivery times depend on the destination, freight method, customs clearance, and shipment requirements. Air freight is generally suitable for urgent shipments, while ocean freight is commonly used for larger cargo.

  • 04. How should I prepare my goods for transportation?

    Answer:

    Pack your goods securely using suitable materials and label each package clearly. Provide accurate shipment details, dimensions, weight, and any special handling instructions to help ensure safe transportation.
